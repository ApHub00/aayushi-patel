Revenue Concentration Risk

MI Challenge · Theme 3 · Stakeholder: CMO · May 2026

Queried a 5-table e-commerce database (50,100 campaign touches · 20,050 orders · 5,020 customers · 25 campaigns) to identify which marketing channels hold a disproportionate share of revenue — and what the CMO must approve before a single-channel failure creates structural business risk.

Ran a full 5-check data quality audit before analysis — surfacing 50 orphan orders and 100 orphan campaign touches — ensuring results were defensible at the executive level.
